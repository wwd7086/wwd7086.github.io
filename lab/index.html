Lab

Four candidate interactive elements for the front page, each shown in place. Not linked from the site. Move the mouse around, stop, leave the window, come back; try them on a phone too.

  • 1 of 4

    The belief cloud

    A particle filter estimating where the cursor is from noisy measurements. Mist that coalesces when you stop and dissolves when you leave.

  • 2 of 4

    The Jansen leg

    A Strandbeest linkage walking on its own; the cursor's horizontal position scrubs the crank.

  • 3 of 4

    The reaching arm

    A three-link arm in the page margin solving inverse kinematics toward the cursor, with weight. Step outside its reach.

  • 4 of 4

    The predicted path

    A Kalman filter predicting the cursor ~1 s ahead with its uncertainty. Move in straight lines, then turn suddenly.
